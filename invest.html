Not investment advice. This is a decision-support tool: it applies a fixed, published set of heuristics to numbers you type in, and shows its whole reasoning so you can disagree with it. It fetches no market data, knows nothing about the current market, and cannot verify a single figure you enter — garbage in, confident-looking garbage out. Scores are opinions encoded as arithmetic, not forecasts. Past performance does not predict future returns. Do your own research and consider a licensed adviser for anything that matters.
