What the workspace computes

The skill supplies inputs only. These outputs are derived here, so the arithmetic is reproducible and every assumption is yours to flex:

  • Discounted cash flow — five explicit years plus a Gordon terminal value, per scenario
  • Asset-base valuation — regulated or invested capital base × a normalised multiple
  • Justified price-to-book from normalised ROE, with a full ROE × cost-of-equity matrix
  • Sum-of-the-parts across segments with different economics
  • Peer median multiples and the subject's discount or premium to them
  • Probability-weighted fair value, expected IRR, and the rating the arithmetic supports
Not investment advice. This workspace renders research produced elsewhere and runs standard valuation arithmetic on inputs it cannot verify. A discounted cash flow is a formula for restating your assumptions with more decimal places, not a source of truth; the terminal value usually carries most of the answer. Labels such as [FACT] record what the researching analyst claimed about a source, not an independent verification of it. Check the primary sources before acting on anything here.
