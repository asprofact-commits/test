Valuation — every method, every scenario

Why the market is wrong — three non-consensus points

  1. Services is decelerating, not compounding. +16% to +12%, and a consensus miss, while cyclical hardware grew 22%. The market pays an annuity multiple for a mix moving toward cyclicality. Phase 3 — Q3 FY2026 Services $30.74bn vs $31.22bn expected.
  2. Low AI capex is dependency, not discipline. Apple rents frontier AI for ~$1bn a year from Google — its largest counterparty, antitrust co-defendant and direct competitor — two years late, with no owned fallback. Phase 2 — the Gemini contract against hyperscaler capex.
  3. The antitrust "win" removed the protection, not the risk. The ~$20bn search payment survived, but is now non-exclusive, capped at 12-month terms, and appealed by both sides. ~13% of operating income at near-100% margin moved to an annual renegotiation. Phase 1 — Judge Mehta's remedy terms.

What would make this call wrong

  • The magnitude is assumption-sensitive. On generous inputs — 4.0% terminal growth, 8.0% WACC — the discount narrows from 40% to 17%. The direction is robust across every reasonable input set; the size is not.
  • 77% of the base-case DCF sits in the terminal value. Most of this valuation is an assumption about the world after year five, which is exactly where a DCF is weakest.
  • Quality is not in question. Apple held price in a falling Chinese market and took share from 13.9% to 18.1%. A great business can stay expensive for a very long time, and this call earns nothing while it does.
  • The bull catalyst is real and imminent. iOS 27 ships full conversational Siri this month. If it drives a genuine upgrade cycle, the bear and base cash-flow paths are both too low.
