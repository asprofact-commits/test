# SpaceX — Phase 4: Capital structure & returns on capital

**Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX)** · NASDAQ: SPCX · $147.95 · ~$1.92tn · 2026-09-05

> **Evidence note.** No primary filing was retrievable. **Net debt is assumed zero** — a declared gap that flatters the valuation slightly rather than harming it.

## The supply event is almost entirely ahead

| Event | Shares | vs IPO float |
|---|---|---|
| IPO float, Jun 2026 | **638.9m** | — |
| Unlocked 6 Aug 2026 | 911.5m | **+43%** |
| **Full expiry, 8 Dec 2026** | Balance of ~13bn | **~19×** |

Roughly **95% of the share base was locked at listing**. All price discovery observed so far has taken place on under 5% of the company. The stock traded *below* its $135 IPO price during July and August, closing above it for the first time in weeks on 10 August.

## Financial character

- **Net loss of $541m** in Q2 2026 despite $3.5bn of adjusted EBITDA
- **Consolidated free cash flow negative** — Starlink's ~$8.1bn of 2026 pro forma FCF is not retained by the group
- **ROIC cannot be measured** — two quarters of reporting, loss-making, a transformational related-party acquisition seven months ago, three asset bases under construction. The central claim of the bull case is currently **unfalsifiable**.

## The Alphabet cross-reference

Alphabet's Q2 2026 results carried a **$98bn net gain** in other income, primarily unrealised marks on **SpaceX** and one private holding — **$77.1bn** of which reached net income after tax.

A third party's mark on its SpaceX stake was large enough to distort the reported earnings of a $4 trillion company. That cuts both ways: it **independently corroborates** that SpaceX's valuation rose steeply through 2026, and it shows how far the consequences of marking this single asset now reach into other companies' reported profits.

## What this phase establishes
1. Loss-making, with negative consolidated free cash flow, funding three programmes at once.
2. Price discovery has occurred on under 5% of the share base; ~12.4bn shares unlock by 8 December.
3. The valuation rose ~5× in twelve months, substantially on a self-valued related-party deal.
4. ROIC is unmeasurable, so the bull case cannot be tested.
5. Alphabet's earnings distortion independently corroborates the markup.

## Could not establish
- Net debt, cash and IPO proceeds (assumed zero)
- Exact share count (~13bn derived; a 10% error moves per-share value 10%)
- Capex and cash burn rate
- Whether an independent fairness process supported the $250bn xAI mark

## What it changes
Supplies a reason for caution beyond the multiple: the price was set on a very small float, by a market that has seen two quarters of numbers, in a security whose tradable supply is about to multiply roughly twentyfold.
