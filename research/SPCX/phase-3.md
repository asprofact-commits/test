# SpaceX — Phase 3: Demand & monetisation structure

**Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX)** · NASDAQ: SPCX · $147.95 · ~$1.92tn · 2026-09-05

> **Evidence note.** No primary filing was retrievable — every external domain is blocked by this environment's egress proxy, including `sec.gov`. Findings rest on search-result summaries. This matters more here than in any other dossier: **SpaceX has been public for under three months and has reported two quarters.**

## The first public quarter was strong

| Measure | Q2 2026 |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **$7.8bn**, +92% YoY (beat $6.81bn consensus) |
| Adjusted EBITDA | **$3.5bn**, +191% |
| **Net income** | **−$541m** |
| Connectivity | $4.3bn, **1.7m net subscriber adds** |
| AI segment | $2.6bn, **+247%** |

Starlink reached **12m subscribers** by mid-2026 (roughly double YoY), projected 16.8m by year end. In FY2025 it produced $11.39bn of revenue — 61% of company sales — and was the **only profitable business** at $4.42bn of segment income. For 2026: ~$20bn revenue, ~$14bn EBITDA, ~63% margin, **~85% recurring**.

Growth is margin-accretive at the EBITDA line (+191% on +92% revenue) — **but not at the net line**, where the company still lost $541m.

## The decisive number

Management projects a **$100bn annualised revenue run rate by end-2026**. Q2 revenue of $7.8bn annualises to **~$31bn**.

That implies roughly **tripling the run rate in two quarters**, and no bridge has been disclosed. Deutsche Bank called it "likely very achievable."

Unlike Alphabet — which could point to **$514bn of contracted backlog** to justify its capital programme — SpaceX has disclosed no equivalent order book.

## What this phase establishes
1. Operating momentum is genuine and rapid.
2. Starlink is a superb business and the only profitable segment.
3. The company still lost $541m at the net line; adjusted EBITDA excludes the costs that matter.
4. The $100bn target is speculative until bridged — and earns zero base-case weight.
5. No backlog disclosure, so the capital programme cannot be classed as demand-backed.

## Could not establish
- **The bridge from $31bn to $100bn** — the single most valuable disclosure the company could make
- Starlink ARPU and churn
- Whether AI segment revenue is third-party or intra-group
- Any order backlog

## What it changes
Draws the line between what is operating and what is claimed. Starlink at 12m subscribers is real, profitable and compounding. The rest is not yet evidenced.
