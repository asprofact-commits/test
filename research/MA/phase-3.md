**Mastercard Incorporated** · NYSE: MA · $580.00 · ~$507bn · 2026-09-05

>
> **Sector adaptation.** Recorded as `financials` to match GICS, but **the banking profile does not apply**: no loan book, no credit risk, no CET1, no net interest margin. Mastercard bears no credit exposure on what it switches — issuers do. Phases 1 and 3 use the software/internet profile (network effects, switching costs, antitrust); Phase 2 uses an infrastructure toll-road frame.

**Almost none of the valuation rests on speculative demand** — the opposite of the Apple, Alphabet and SpaceX dossiers. This business is priced on cash it already generates.

The corresponding weakness: revenue is **recurring by behaviour, not by contract**. There is no $514bn backlog as Alphabet has. The risk is not that promised demand fails to arrive — it is that *existing* demand migrates to another rail.

## What this phase establishes
1. FY2025 revenue +16% with margin +230bp; Q2 2026 +14% with an EPS beat and raised guidance.
2. VAS at ~41% of revenue growing 20% — genuine diversification and a partial answer to substitution.
3. Growth is margin-accretive, the cleanest case in this series.
4. Almost no speculative content in the base case.
5. But no contracted backlog — recurring by behaviour, which is exactly what the settlement and substitution target.

## What it changes
A rare case where the base case needs almost no speculative content. Consequence for Phase 5: **the DCF becomes the most trustworthy instrument here rather than the least** — the reverse of every other company in this series.
