Revenue growth **accelerated** from 15% to a consensus 21% while operating income grew 30% in both reported quarters. At this scale that combination is unusual.

## Cloud is the engine, and it compounds profitably

$24.8bn in Q2, **+82%**, with operating income more than tripling to $8.8bn and margin from 20.7% to **35.6%**. Behind it sits **$514bn of backlog** — more than four times total company revenue and roughly nine times annualised Cloud revenue.

The fastest-growing segment carries the highest incremental margin. That is the **opposite** of the volume-without-value trap this protocol exists to catch.

## The AI-kills-Search thesis is not visible in the numbers

Search **+17%** to $63.3bn. YouTube ads $11.06bn against $9.79bn. Two years after the disruption was first predicted, the core franchise is accelerating.

## What this phase establishes
1. Revenue accelerating 15% → ~21% with operating income +30%.
2. Cloud compounding at 82% with margin expanding, behind $514bn of contracted backlog.
3. No visible AI cannibalisation of Search.
4. Growth is accretive at every level examined.
5. Most of the demand is contracted, not narrative.
