## Reverse DCF

At a 9.0% WACC the price implies **~5.1% perpetual growth** — above long-run nominal GDP (~4%). At 3.5% terminal growth it implies a **~7.6% WACC**.

Demanding, but **materially less so than Apple's 5.7% and 6.9%** — on roughly 21% revenue growth versus Apple's ~15%.

# Investment Committee Memo

**Rating: `HOLD / NEUTRAL`**

**A note on the rating, because the model disagrees.** The arithmetic returns **AVOID at −10.7%**, 0.7 percentage points past the HOLD boundary. I am stating HOLD because a 100bp change in WACC moves fair value ~15% — the model cannot distinguish −10.7% from −9.9%, and letting a threshold manufacture that precision would be false.

This is the **opposite call from the Apple dossier**, where I moved my rating *to* the arithmetic. There the gap was −32% and even the bull case was −15%: outside any error bar. Here the bull case is positive and expected IRR is positive. Different facts, different treatment.

## Why the market is mispricing this — 3 non-consensus points

1. **Market believes:** Alphabet is the cheapest megacap at ~17× earnings.
   **We believe:** That multiple is computed on trailing earnings containing **$77.1bn of after-tax unrealised gains** on SpaceX and one private holding. Clean, it is ~29.8× — at the peer median, not below it. *The single most common reason given for owning this stock does not survive checking.*
   *Evidence — Phase 4: Alphabet's own disclosure that the gain added $6.26 to the $9.11 reported Q2 EPS, leaving $2.85 against a $2.88 consensus.*

2. **Market believes:** The AI capex boom threatens Alphabet's cash generation.
   **We believe:** Alphabet is the only hyperscaler that can already show where the money converts — Cloud +82% with operating margin **20.7% → 35.6%**, behind **$514bn of contracted backlog**. This is a compensated build against contracted demand, which is why the bottleneck is capacity rather than customers.
   *Evidence — Phases 2–3: Q2 2026 Cloud segment disclosures and the backlog figure.*

3. **Market believes:** Antitrust is an unquantifiable overhang.
   **We believe:** At district level it resolved, and resolved well. No Chrome divestiture, no Android breakup, and on **2 Sept 2026 Brinkema rejected all three DOJ structural remedies** in ad tech. What remains is conduct regulation and an appeal — and the same 12-month rule that repriced Apple's biggest profit line capped Alphabet's biggest single cost.
   *Evidence — Phase 1: Mehta remedy terms; Brinkema ruling 2026-09-02.*

## What the consensus is correctly terrified of

1. **Free cash flow has gone negative for the first time since the 2004 IPO**, buybacks are halted, and Alphabet is funding a $200bn-a-year build with debt and the largest equity raise in US corporate history — while still paying a dividend. A genuine change in financial character, in one year.
2. **~60% of capex is short-lived compute and the useful-life assumption is unverifiable from outside.** Six-year to four-year lives would cut reported earnings by tens of billions with no change in the business — and it sits directly under the two methods carrying 80% of the valuation weight.
3. **FY2027 capex consensus is ~$257bn, 28% above an already-doubled 2026, with no stated peak.** Open-ended capital commitment against partly-trusted demand is how capital cycles end badly — and the whole sector is doing it simultaneously.

## Trigger-to-sell

- Cloud revenue growth **below 35%** YoY for two consecutive quarters
- Consolidated operating margin **below 30%** for two consecutive quarters on rising depreciation
- FY2027 capex guided **above $280bn** without a corresponding rise in Cloud backlog
- Search revenue growth **below 8%** YoY in any quarter
- The D.C. Circuit **revives a structural remedy** in either case
- Clean P/E on operating earnings **above 35×**

**Re-entry:** fair value ~$302. A 15% margin of safety implies adding below **~$257**; the base case supports paying up to ~$300. With expected three-year IRR of ~+3.5%, an existing holder has no reason to sell on valuation alone — **this is a do-not-chase call, not a get-out call**.
