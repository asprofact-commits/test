# Alphabet — Phase 1: Pricing-power architecture

**Alphabet Inc.** · NASDAQ: GOOGL · $338.72 · market cap $4.1tn · 2026-09-05

Findings rest on search-result summaries of Alphabet's 8-K/10-Q releases and reputable press. See `.claude/skills/invest/reference/network-access.md` for the fix.

## Who sets the price

Nobody publishes one. Search advertising is priced by **continuous auction**, so pricing power shows up as auction density rather than a rate card — and the auction is still tightening. **Search revenue grew 17% to $63.3bn in Q2 2026**, two years after generative AI was first predicted to erode it.

Cloud pricing is contractual and improving fast: **operating margin rose from 20.7% to 35.6% while revenue grew 82%**. Margin expanding *with* volume is the signature of real pricing power, not share bought with discount.

## The antitrust overhang has largely resolved — favourably

| Case | Structural risk | Outcome | Live? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search** (Mehta) | Chrome divestiture, Android breakup, payment ban | All declined. Exclusivity and tying limited; Apple payments allowed but capped at **12-month terms** | Yes — D.C. Circuit, both sides appealing |
| **Ad tech** (Brinkema) | AdX divestiture, DFP auction open-sourced | **All three structural remedies rejected, 2 Sept 2026.** Behavioural remedies only | Yes — Google appealing liability |

The break-up scenario that hung over this equity for two years did not happen at district level in either case. Google keeps Chrome, Android, the ad stack and its distribution deals.

Two things remain live: the D.C. Circuit appeal (with the DOJ cross-appealing for *stronger* remedies), and the sealed text of Brinkema's behavioural remedies — due unsealed ~16 Sept, with a Final Judgment by **2 October 2026**.

## The mirror image of the Apple dossier

The same court order that repriced Apple's largest **profit** line repriced Alphabet's largest single **cost** line. Alphabet pays Apple ~$20bn a year — roughly 12% of estimated FY2026 operating income — and must now **re-win it annually and cannot buy exclusivity at any price**.

Arguably the 12-month rule is worse for Alphabet than for Apple: Apple gains an annual auction it can run; Alphabet must keep winning one.

## Competition

Moving in Alphabet's favour through 2026. ChatGPT's share of AI assistants **fell below 50% for the first time** (from 87.2% two years earlier) while Gemini rose above 21%; Gemini's share of chatbot web traffic went **5.7% → 27.7%**. Gemini has ~662m monthly users against ChatGPT's 1.1bn — second in assistants, still first in search.

## What this phase establishes
1. Pricing power is demonstrated, not asserted — Search +17%, Cloud margin 20.7%→35.6% on 82% growth.
2. The legal overhang that justified a structurally cheap multiple has largely resolved in Alphabet's favour.
3. Conduct risk remains; existence risk has gone. Both cases are still on appeal.
4. Alphabet's biggest single cost is now repriced annually with no exclusivity available.
5. Dual-class control means minorities carry the economics without governance recourse.
