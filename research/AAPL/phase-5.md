## The reverse DCF — the honest statement of the problem

Rather than argue about my assumptions, invert the question: *what must be true to justify $328?*

- At an **8.8% WACC**, terminal growth must be **~5.7% in perpetuity** — above long-run US nominal GDP (~4%). The price embeds Apple growing faster than the economy, forever.
- Holding terminal growth at a defensible **3.5%**, the price requires a **~6.9% WACC** — an equity risk premium of ~2.7%. The market is pricing Apple closer to a bond than an equity.

## Honest caveat

**77% of the base-case DCF value sits in the terminal assumption.** On generous inputs (4.0% terminal growth, 8.0% WACC) the DCF gives ~$272 — a 17% discount, not 40%. **The direction is robust across every reasonable input set; the magnitude is not.** Anyone who rejects 3.0% terminal growth should flex it in the workspace and watch the answer move.

## Why the market is mispricing this — 3 non-consensus points

1. **Market believes:** Services is the durable annuity justifying the premium.
   **We believe:** Services decelerated +16% → +12% and *missed* consensus, while hardware — the cyclical line — grew 22%. An annuity multiple is being paid for a mix moving toward cyclicality.
   *Evidence: Phase 3, Q2 vs Q3 FY2026 Services growth and the $30.74bn vs $31.22bn miss.*

2. **Market believes:** Low AI capex is discipline that will let Apple out-earn the hyperscalers.
   **We believe:** Apple is renting its most strategically important capability for ~$1bn/yr from its largest counterparty, antitrust co-defendant and direct competitor — two years late, with no owned fallback. That is dependency, not discipline.
   *Evidence: Phase 2, the Gemini contract against hyperscaler capex; Siri shipping Sept 2026 after a 2024 demo.*

3. **Market believes:** The antitrust remedy was a win because the $20bn survived.
   **We believe:** The payment survived; its protection did not. Non-exclusive, no restriction on promoting rivals, 12-month cap, both sides appealing. ~13% of operating income at near-100% margin moved from a locked contract to an annual renegotiation.
   *Evidence: Phase 1, Mehta remedy terms; Google's May 2026 and DOJ's Feb 2026 appeals.*

## What the consensus is correctly terrified of

1. **The valuation genuinely requires perpetual above-GDP growth.** Not a bear talking point — it is what the reverse DCF returns, across every reasonable input set.
2. **FY2026 is a replacement cycle, and cycles pull demand forward.** Q4 guidance already steps down to +9–11% from +16%. Two quarters of +22% iPhone growth is what a pull-forward looks like before it becomes an air pocket.
3. **~$20bn of near-costless profit rests on one annually-repriced contract** with a company that is both counterparty and competitor. Unreplaceable by pricing; neither appeal resolves for years.
