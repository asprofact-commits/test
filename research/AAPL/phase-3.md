Net income ~**$131bn** (+17%). Growth is **accretive** — Q3 net income grew 27% on 16% revenue. This is the opposite of the volume-without-value trap.

## But the composition is wrong for the story being paid for

**Services decelerated from +16% to +12% and missed consensus** ($30.74bn vs $31.22bn) — while iPhone, the *cyclical* line, held at +22%. The market pays an annuity multiple for Services; the mix is currently moving toward cyclicality, not away from it.

And roughly **16% of Services revenue is the Google payment** (~$20bn of ~$125.6bn), carrying a much higher share of Services *profit*. Services growth and Services quality are not the same question.

## What this phase establishes
1. ~$477bn revenue (+14.7%), ~$131bn net income (+17%) — an outstanding year.
2. Growth is currently margin-accretive.
3. The high-margin annuity is decelerating while the cyclical line carries growth.
4. ~16% of Services revenue is one annually-repriced contract.
5. The supercycle is speculative and earns zero base-case weight.

## Could not establish
- Services growth **ex-Google** — the number that actually matters, not disclosed
- Whether the iPhone 17 cycle pulled FY2027 demand forward
- Why Services decelerated and missed

## What it changes
Apple is not expensive because it isn't growing. It is expensive because the market capitalises a **cyclical hardware surge and a decelerating Services line** as though both were permanent.
